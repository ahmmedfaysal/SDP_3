# EduNexus - A central point for study resources.

**EduNexus** s a platform designed to connect students and provide a seamless solution for sharing and accessing class notes. New students often face challenges in obtaining high-quality notes, and this platform bridges the gap by enabling both new and old students to upload, access, and evaluate notes. The system identifies and ranks the best notes based on quality and relevance, ensuring that all students benefit from shared resources. Each user has a personalized account that allows them to sign in, sign out, and update their information on this platform. Also they can  contribute to the platform with notes and resources.
